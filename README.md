## About

I like taking a product from the very first schema to what actually runs in production, as one connected system rather than a pile of disconnected parts.

Right now I am founding and leading engineering at **PappaChat**, where we move everyday business workflows like customer service, bookings, sales and shipping onto hybrid LLM pipelines. A lot of my work sits at the point where messy real-world operations meet clean, reliable software: real-time event-driven systems, retrieval and multi-agent orchestration, and GDPR-compliant EU data residency across both commercial cloud APIs and self-hosted local models.

I question what "real-world" actually means, and then I build for it.

**Currently:** CTO & Founder @ PappaChat · Full-Stack Developer @ CrowdM

## What I Do

- **Ship full products end to end.** Schema design, APIs, frontend, authentication, billing and AI features, built as a single coherent system.
- **Architect AI-native systems.** Hybrid LLM pipelines, RAG, vector search, tool and function calling, MCP, and multi-agent orchestration in real production settings.
- **Own the whole delivery pipeline.** From local development to production: Git workflows, infrastructure setup, automated deployments and day-to-day production operations.
- **Turn product into systems.** I translate business requirements into scalable, maintainable software, connecting product thinking with engineering execution.
- **Think about the business, not just the code.** Pricing and subscription models, revenue architecture, positioning, and investor-facing product readiness.
- **Design for scale.** Performance-minded architecture and infrastructure that stays healthy as usage grows.
